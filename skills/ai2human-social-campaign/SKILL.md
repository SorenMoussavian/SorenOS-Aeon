# AI2Human Social Campaign

This skill is isolated from natural human-execution tasks.

## Required Inputs

- Exact X post, handle, Telegram/community URL, or submission target.
- Requested action and platform rules.
- Eligibility, winner count, deadline, proof method, and anti-abuse rules.
- Funding mode and recorded pool/escrow before paid publication.

## Hard Rules

- Never infer social actions from a generic digital task.
- Never invent campaign links.
- Never display a paid reward when funding is absent.
- Refuse fake reviews, spam, coordinated deception, credential handling, or platform abuse.

Use campaign-specific templates only after all inputs and funding checks pass.

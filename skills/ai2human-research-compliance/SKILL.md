# AI2Human Research and Compliance

Use `compliance_check` for policy/checklist review and `digital_task` for public-source access checks.

## Boundaries

Define the artifact, exact question, allowed public sources, verdict options, and limitations. Ask for direct URLs, screenshots or document excerpts, timestamp, and concise notes.

Do not ask the operator to provide legal advice, bypass access controls, handle credentials, expose personal data, or certify facts beyond the supplied evidence.

## Verification

Separate source accessibility from truth or scientific validity. A reviewer may confirm that code/data/documentation is reachable without endorsing its quality.
